# Journey atmosphere transitions

The validated `corner-waves.html` and `mobile-sea-descent.html` supply the visual choreography and transparent artwork. Their playback controls, synthetic scrolling, canvases and photographic backgrounds are not shipped. Existing resort photos, palette tokens, text, typography, card sizes and interactions are retained.

## Integration

The three gradient spacer elements and their CSS are removed. Two native sticky paint viewports occupy zero net flow height (each height is cancelled by its negative margin). Existing level photos are portaled into the lower viewport and crossfade together with their original colors. Their current loading/readiness and 10% photo opacity are preserved. Section and card dimensions stay unchanged; only the former spacer height is removed. A rectangular clip bounds the zero-flow sticky surface at the last station, preventing its paint from spilling over the footer without creating another scroll container. At its first edge only, a 36–64px foreground bleed announces HEAVEN slightly before its section enters; the photo plane remains bounded and no flow height is added.

`useJourneyMotion` supplies the existing passive, coalesced scroll clock. Section positions are measured on layout changes, including real fractional offsets and the existing arrival scroll margin. Progress is recalculated from scrolling, including reversals and fast skips; no wheel/touch interception, scroll lock or animation queue is introduced. The Nam Tram and existing docking assistance remain intact.

HEAVEN starts 36–64px before its section enters the viewport, once cloud images are ready. The native scroll opens the clouds and brings the warm illumination; reversing the approach returns to the same pose, and stopping leaves a static pose without a clock loop. At alignment it finishes over 900 ms; a direct/cold arrival without an approach gets a gentle 2.1-second entrance. Completion ends only the current visit. Returning through HEAVEN from either direction or selecting it again in Nam Tram rearms its entrance, including an already-centred level. The existing navigation signal wakes the shared scheduler even if there is no scroll event. Nam Tram starts its jump from the sheet close-completion callback, after native scroll locking is released, rather than during its exit animation. There is no persistent one-shot flag or idle replay loop; a downward departure beyond the centred station immediately cancels HEAVEN’s clouds/light so SKY alone takes over. Upward returns into HEAVEN remain replayable. An image load wakes the existing scheduler after a cold stop. SKY uses exactly three separately cropped birds (bee-eater, drongo, sunbird). EARTH uses two branches, one liana, a fern and three individual leaves. An exponential follow (60ms desktop, 90ms below 768px) smooths only their decorative transforms/opacity across coarse wheel or touch steps, using actual elapsed frame time (bounded to 160ms) even on slower renderers, with no overshoot; it settles to an exact idle pose and uses the existing RAF scheduler. Below 768px, its path uses the stable level layout height rather than the toolbar-sensitive innerHeight. A height-only refresh preserves the in-flight pose; width changes, distant navigation and leaving the effect reset it. Desktop geometry and response stay unchanged. Its arrival movement eases rather than changing speed abruptly. SEA uses four small waves, with scales 0.48/1/0.67/0.83, on rows measured between EARTH’s actual pagination and SEA’s subtitle. Every wave crosses the full viewport from right to left, remains visible through the left edge, then fades; the arrival tail is 850ms rather than 1800ms. They have different sizes/delays and all travel right to left, continuing briefly on arrival before fading. The large centred/held illustration is removed. The card anchor is cached on real layout/viewport changes, never read during a paint frame. At the user’s revised request, all decorations sit above titles/cards; photos stay below, and both planes ignore pointer input. No content or card footprint changes. SKY/EARTH tails remain 950/750 ms.

No continuous idle loop runs. Hidden tabs and offscreen journeys clear pending tails. Reduced motion uses the original static section palette/photos and does not request decorative assets. Semantic controls, focus indicators and card interactions are unchanged; decoration is aria-hidden and cannot intercept pointers. A ready guard keeps native readable section backgrounds if the enhancement has not initialized.

## Delivery size and image handling

Ten separate transparent WebP files plus one SVG add **86,446 bytes**: birds 25,604; plants/leaves 59,536; waves 1,306 (682 gzip). The enlarged raster and subsequent large wave strip have been replaced by a single small crest SVG, reused by all four currents in the same turquoise/ivory direction. The backlit cloud and other cutouts remain unchanged. The waves have no animated rotation or filter, use separate heights, and fade as they leave to the left. HEAVEN cloud anchors have a small bounded lead above the section (including mobile); a localized warm light with normal blending stays visible on the ivory desktop palette. Both layers remain visible together. Reference crop rectangles and polygon separation are applied offline, with no runtime canvas. The cloud is byte-identical to the existing `/hero-cloud-ivory-a26e472d.webp` (SHA-256 `a26e472dfd8cb5fcb4f34a19dd2ea8bdb6a4373b17dfea2e69fdedc3f4c82c46`) and reuses that file/cache entry.

Imports explicitly use Vite `?no-inline`, including the small leaves: no embedded image payload is added to JavaScript. Source assignment is deferred until approach, at low priority; intentional Nam Tram jumps only admit their requested effects. New decorative requests wait until the approaching level has admitted and settled its front photo, and other admitted high-priority front photos are settled. Already requested hero clouds are reused. Load/error wakes admission after a stationary wait. Save-Data or slow-2g/2g skips decorations altogether while keeping existing colors/photos and navigation. A deliberately blocked-photo test confirms that new artwork waits, then starts without another scroll when the photo loads. These guards preserve content priority; they do not assert a universal zero-network-contention guarantee. Intrinsic dimensions are supplied. No dependency, video, GIF or new photographic background is added.

The production build adds approximately **4.7 KB gzip of JS/CSS** compared with main `87258a3`. Initial hero checks observe **zero new decorative image requests**. The readiness observer ignores edge-only intersections, preventing a just-departed chapter from warming at the shared seam in Safari.
