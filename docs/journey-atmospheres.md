# Journey atmosphere transitions

The validated `corner-waves.html` and `mobile-sea-descent.html` supply the visual choreography and transparent artwork. Their playback controls, synthetic scrolling, canvases and photographic backgrounds are not shipped. Existing resort photos, palette tokens, text, typography, card sizes and interactions are retained.

## Integration

The three gradient spacer elements and their CSS are removed. Two native sticky paint viewports occupy zero net flow height (each height is cancelled by its negative margin). Existing level photos are portaled into the lower viewport and crossfade together with their original colors. Their current loading/readiness and 10% photo opacity are preserved. Section and card dimensions stay unchanged; only the former spacer height is removed. A rectangular clip bounds the zero-flow sticky surface at the last station, preventing its paint from spilling over the footer without creating another scroll container.

`useJourneyMotion` supplies the existing passive, coalesced scroll clock. Section positions are measured on layout changes, including real fractional offsets and the existing arrival scroll margin. Progress is recalculated from scrolling, including reversals and fast skips; no wheel/touch interception, scroll lock or animation queue is introduced. The Nam Tram and existing docking assistance remain intact.

HEAVEN now starts when the first part of its section enters the viewport, once cloud images are ready. The native scroll opens the clouds and brings the warm illumination; reversing the approach returns to the same pose, and stopping leaves a static pose without a clock loop. At alignment it finishes over 900 ms; a direct/cold arrival without an approach gets a gentle 2.1-second entrance. Completion consumes this one-shot entrance. An image load wakes the existing scheduler after a cold stop. SKY uses exactly three separately cropped birds (bee-eater, drongo, sunbird). EARTH uses two branches, one liana, a fern and three individual leaves. SEA travels from right to left across the real front card's vertical centre. The complete SVG fits inside the viewport, including phones. On arrival it holds for about 900 ms, then fades in place over 900 ms. The card anchor is cached on real layout/viewport changes, never read during a paint frame. At the user’s revised request, all decorations sit above titles/cards; photos stay below, and both planes ignore pointer input. No content or card footprint changes. SKY/EARTH tails remain 950/750 ms.

No continuous idle loop runs. Hidden tabs and offscreen journeys clear pending tails. Reduced motion uses the original static section palette/photos and does not request decorative assets. Semantic controls, focus indicators and card interactions are unchanged; decoration is aria-hidden and cannot intercept pointers. A ready guard keeps native readable section backgrounds if the enhancement has not initialized.

## Delivery size and image handling

Ten separate transparent WebP files plus one SVG add **88,702 bytes**: birds 25,604; plants/leaves 59,536; waves 3,562 (1,630 gzip). The enlarged 960px raster wave was replaced by a clean vector interpretation of the validated three-crest illustration, using the same turquoise/ivory direction. The backlit cloud and other cutouts remain unchanged. The wave has no animated rotation, is fitted to the real card, and fades after its short hold instead of moving back out through the corner. Reference crop rectangles and polygon separation are applied offline, with no runtime canvas. The cloud is byte-identical to the existing `/hero-cloud-ivory-a26e472d.webp` (SHA-256 `a26e472dfd8cb5fcb4f34a19dd2ea8bdb6a4373b17dfea2e69fdedc3f4c82c46`) and reuses that file/cache entry.

Imports explicitly use Vite `?no-inline`, including the small leaves: no embedded image payload is added to JavaScript. Source assignment is deferred until approach, at low priority; intentional Nam Tram jumps only admit their requested effects. New decorative requests wait until the approaching level has admitted and settled its front photo, and other admitted high-priority front photos are settled. Already requested hero clouds are reused. Load/error wakes admission after a stationary wait. Save-Data or slow-2g/2g skips decorations altogether while keeping existing colors/photos and navigation. A deliberately blocked-photo test confirms that new artwork waits, then starts without another scroll when the photo loads. These guards preserve content priority; they do not assert a universal zero-network-contention guarantee. Intrinsic dimensions are supplied. No dependency, video, GIF or new photographic background is added.

The production build adds approximately **4.0 KB gzip of JS/CSS** compared with main `87258a3`. Initial hero checks observe **zero new decorative image requests**. The readiness observer ignores edge-only intersections, preventing a just-departed chapter from warming at the shared seam in Safari.
